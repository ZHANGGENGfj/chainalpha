# Evidence Policy

## Authority

1. Exchange, regulator, audited filing, and company disclosure.
2. Structured market or financial API with an explicit observation date.
3. Mainstream financial media quoting a named source.
4. Broker research with report date and institution.
5. Social media and unattributed commentary.

## Required fields

Every retained item should include title, source, URL or local path, publication time, as-of date when applicable, and an excerpt supporting the claim.

## Verification

- Exact financial values: verify period, unit, currency, and consolidation scope.
- Price changes: verify start/end timestamps, adjusted-price convention, and corporate actions.
- Customer/supplier claims: require a filing, named company statement, or at least two independent credible sources.
- Forecasts: label source, forecast date, assumptions, and whether the number is consensus or a single institution.
